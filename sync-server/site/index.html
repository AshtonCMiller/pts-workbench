One repair order, six places to look

Every job means jumping between the repair order system, the service manual, bulletins, wiring diagrams, diagnostics and a notepad for your hours, and typing the same VIN into each of them.

What it does

Everything for the job, in the bay

Opens to the right vehicle

Reads the repair orders assigned to you and opens each vehicle's manual, bulletins and wiring by itself. No more typing VINs.

Diagnostics in one place

Trouble codes, the vehicle's health history and software updates, vehicle history and labor times, side by side with the manual.

Your hours, kept for you

Flagged hours for every pay period, checked against your pay sheet so nothing goes missing, plus spiffs you've turned in.

Never miss an approval

Know the moment repairs are approved or declined, or the advisor sends a message, even when you've stepped away.

Private by design

Your notes and hours follow you between computers, encrypted before they leave. Customer information never leaves the shop.

Installs without IT

Runs on the shop computers you already have, with no admin rights, and keeps itself up to date in the background.

PTS Workbench

On your phone

Add it to your Home Screen

On an iPhone, notifications only work once PTS Workbench is on your Home Screen.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (in the … menu on newer iPhones).
  2. Pick Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open PTS Workbench from your Home Screen, and scan the code on your computer again.

Pair with your computer

In PTS Workbench on your computer, click Pair phone at the bottom right, then scan the code it shows.

Point the camera at the code on your computer.

Type it in

The same as in the app's Settings on your computer.

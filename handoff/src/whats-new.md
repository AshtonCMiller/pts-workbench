## The new PTS Workbench

# Improvements
- **A brand new PTS Workbench:** Restarting moves you to the new version of the app. It starts faster, uses much
  less of the computer, and the Service Manual opens right inside it. It takes about a minute, once.
- **Everything comes with you:** Your settings and hours stay as they are. You may need to sign in to Xtime and PTS
  again the first time.
